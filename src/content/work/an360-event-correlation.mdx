Operations teams were drowning in tickets: one underlying fault at a cell site produced many related network events, and each one opened its own ticket. People spent time working out which ticket mattered and which were echoes of it.

The goal was to ingest the events as they happen, decide which one is the cause, and give the team one ticket for the whole story. This page describes the approach generically. It contains no internal system names beyond my public resume, no data and no code.
